URL Risk Analysis System

PhishGuard analyses every URL through a seven-stage pipeline — from raw input all the way to a human-readable safety recommendation — so you know exactly why a link is flagged, not just that it was flagged.

{# ── Pipeline stages ── #}

How the Pipeline Works

1

Input Detection

Accept a URL typed directly, or a QR code image (PNG / JPG / GIF). When a QR image is uploaded it is decoded to extract the embedded URL before any further analysis begins.

2

Validate & Normalise

The URL is checked for basic structural validity. It is then normalised: a missing https:// scheme is added, the host is lowercased, default ports are stripped, and common tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid, etc.) are removed.

3

Redirect Resolution

The normalised URL is followed through up to five HTTP redirects to find the true destination — unmasking link-shorteners and multi-hop cloaking chains. The full redirect chain is recorded and shown on the result page.

4

URL Feature Extraction

Over a dozen structural features are extracted from the final URL: domain entropy, suspicious TLDs, raw-IP hosts, brand-name keywords in the subdomain, Punycode / IDN homograph detection, URL length, query-parameter count, executable-file extensions, and more.

5

Contextual Analysis

Contextual signals are layered on top of structural features: cross-domain redirect chains, brand impersonation (e.g. a URL that mentions "paypal" but the domain is not paypal.com), credential-harvesting path patterns, and unencrypted HTTP links on sensitive domains.

6

Feature Fusion & Risk Scoring

URL structural features (55% weight) and contextual signals (45% weight) are combined into a single fused risk score. Hard-boost rules override the weighted score for definitive indicators such as IP-based hosts or confirmed brand impersonation. The score is then classified: Safe (<35%), Suspicious (35–65%), or Malicious (>65%).

7

Safety Recommendation

A plain-English recommendation is generated based on the classification and any triggered signals — explaining exactly what was found and advising whether to proceed, verify independently, or avoid the link entirely.

{# ── Classification thresholds ── #}

Risk Classification

✅
Safe Score < 35%

No significant phishing indicators. Proceed with normal caution.

⚠️
Suspicious Score 35–65%

Some risk signals present. Verify the destination before entering any credentials.

🚨
Malicious Score > 65%

Strong phishing or malware indicators. Avoid opening this link.
