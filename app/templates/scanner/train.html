Expected CSV Format

Your CSV must have at least two columns — one for the URL and one for the label. Column names are detected automatically but can be overridden below.

Label ValueMaps to
safe / legitimate / benign / 0✅ Safe
suspicious / phishing / 1⚠️ Suspicious
malicious / malware / 2🚨 Malicious

Binary datasets (0 = safe, 1 = phishing) are also accepted — 1 maps to Malicious.

Example CSV:

url,label
https://github.com/features,safe
http://paypal-verify.tk/login,phishing
https://google.com,legitimate
http://192.168.1.1/update.exe,malicious
