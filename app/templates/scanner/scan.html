How it works

  • Input — paste a URL directly or upload an image of a QR code.
  • Normalise — the URL is cleaned: scheme added, tracking params stripped.
  • Redirect resolution — shortened or redirecting links are followed to their final destination.
  • Feature extraction — domain entropy, suspicious TLDs, keyword hits, IP hosts, and more are analysed.
  • Contextual analysis — redirect chains, brand impersonation, and credential-harvesting paths are checked.
  • Risk classification — features are fused and scored: Safe, Suspicious, or Malicious.
  • Results are saved to your history.
